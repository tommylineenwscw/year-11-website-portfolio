Low Poly Character

Project Overview

For This Project, I will be making a low poly character model in Blender, as well as rigging and animating it so it can move around and do certain actions. This is to get into a game that we will make in groups later in the year. My Character is going to be a very chubby roundish person, with a big chest, arms, head and stomach area but small legs in comparison.

I will be using Blender to create my character, I will be using the rigging tools to make my character able to move around and do certain actions. I will be using the animation tools to make my character able to do actions such as idling.

End Users

The end users for my low poly animated character are potential employers in the fields of game development, animation, and interactive media who are evaluating my technical and creative capabilities. This project is designed to showcase my proficiency in 3D modeling, rigging, and animation within Blender, as well as my understanding of stylized visual aesthetics. These employers will want to see that i can create a good designed character with rigging and animation. They will also be looking for evidence of creativity and attention to detail. By creating a good and creative low poly character with animation I aim to meet these needs and show that I am capable of doing such projects.

Requirements and Specifications

The character I am going to create is a low poly character, which means it will have a low polygon count and a simple design. The character will be rigged so it can move around and do certain actions. The character will be animated so it can do certain actions such as idling. The character will be created in Blender, and the final product will be exported as an FBX file for use in a game engine. The character must be in a low poly style. This means that I will only use simple shapes with low numbers of faces and Use no detailed textures. It will be a clean and simple model, so I will only model what is needed and no extra details. The model should be easy to rig and animate, which means it will be have its arms out to the side to ensure so, as well as avoiding messy geometry that will bend badly. It must have a clear and orignial design. So I will have a simple but creative idea and the design should be easy to recognise from the silhouette.

Relevant Implications

Aesthetics

Aesthetics are the way my character looks overall. I chose to make the character wide and chubby to make it look simple and goofy, and the small legs in contrast to the massive wide body create a unique and funny look, as well as making a very recognisable silhouette. This helps players instantly recognise my character. And I will keep this silhouette clear and exaggerated, especially the contrast between the large body and tiny legs. I will also avoid too many small details so the round shape still stands out.

Usability

Usability affects how easy players can understand and interact with my character. This is important because I want my character to be as simple to understand as possible. Players can easily interact with my character because I made the design very simple. I will do this by making the rig very simple, only a few bones in the legs and arms to match the low poly style. I will also ensure that the character can be easily selected, moved and animated in Blender or game engines. I will also set a clear origin point centered at the feet for easy placing in scenes.

Functionality

Functionality is how well my character can perform its intended actions. I made my character able to move around and do certain actions, such as jumping and punching. This is important because I want my character to be able to do certain actions in the game I will develop later.
